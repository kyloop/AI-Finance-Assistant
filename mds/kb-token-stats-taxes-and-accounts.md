# Knowledge-base token statistics: taxes-and-accounts

Measurements of the `taxes-and-accounts` source pages, a retrieval test of two chunk limits and an answer-quality test of the same
limits. The design these numbers feed into is [rag-data-design.md](rag-data-design.md); all categories side by side:
[kb-eval-summary.md](kb-eval-summary.md). Generated by `python -m scripts.kb_report --category taxes-and-accounts` from the
CSVs in [data/](data/); only the notes and findings are written by hand.

<!-- notes:start -->
**First measurement (2026-11-24):** 21 pages, the original 15 plus six added for questions about a person's own
income tax (Property tax, State and local tax deduction, Itemized deduction, Federal Insurance Contributions Act, Tax rate,
Alternative minimum tax). "Self-employment tax" and "Net investment income tax" were checked but resolve to general pages
(Self-employment; Affordable Care Act tax provisions), so they were left out; the NIIT is covered by Capital gains tax in
the United States › Additional taxes, and self-employment tax by FICA › Self-employed people.
Questions: q01–q20 were written from the pages, q21–q22 are out of scope. Indexed the same day (`rag.categories`).
<!-- notes:end -->

- **Sample:** the 21 `taxes-and-accounts` pages in [kb_topics.yaml](../src/data/kb_topics.yaml), full Wikipedia text (not
  truncated), saved in [tests/data/kb_snapshot/taxes-and-accounts/](../tests/data/kb_snapshot/taxes-and-accounts/).
- **Tokenizer:** `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, the embedding model, without `[CLS]`/`[SEP]`.
- **Raw vs clean:** "raw" is the text as `WikiClient.get_page()` returns it. "Clean" has each MathML formula dump collapsed
  to inline LaTeX; all section and paragraph figures use clean text.
- **Section** = text under one heading (`##`–`#####`), excluding its subsections. **Paragraph** = one line of the source
  text; list items count as separate paragraphs.

Data: [taxes-and-accounts_docs.csv](data/taxes-and-accounts_docs.csv), [taxes-and-accounts_sections.csv](data/taxes-and-accounts_sections.csv), [taxes-and-accounts_paragraphs.csv](data/taxes-and-accounts_paragraphs.csv).

## Generation test: answer quality

`python -m scripts.eval_generation --category taxes-and-accounts`: for each question and limit, the top-5 chunks go to `gpt-4o-mini`
(the app's model, temperature 0) with the instruction to answer only from them (≤ 150 words) or say they don't cover
the question. `gpt-4o-mini` then judges the answer against the chunks and the question's `reference` / `key_points`.
Because the same model writes and judges, scores may be lenient.

| Metric | Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | 1–5 | Does the answer address the question asked? |
| Faithfulness | 0–1 | Share of the answer's claims supported by the retrieved chunks (hallucination check); not scored for refusals |
| Correctness | 1–5 | Do the answer's facts agree with the reference answer? |
| Completeness | 0–1 | Share of the reference key points covered; 0 for a refused answerable question |

Refusals ("the sources don't cover this") are detected from the answer text. Raw results, with every answer, unsupported
claim and judge reason: [eval_taxes-and-accounts_generation.csv](data/eval_taxes-and-accounts_generation.csv), [eval_taxes-and-accounts_generation_summary.csv](data/eval_taxes-and-accounts_generation_summary.csv).

## Findings

<!-- findings:start -->
- Retrieval is strong: 20 of 20 answerable questions find an expected section in the top 5 at both limits, 18 at rank 1.
- Answers are relevant (5.00) and correct (4.90 at max 250); faithfulness 0.95, a little below the earlier categories,
  mostly from answers adding a general sentence the chunks don't state.
- The two out-of-scope questions score 0.682 and 0.710, above `rag.min_score` (0.68), as in bonds: the verifier's
  LLM check is what turns them away. Both were declined in the generation test.
- Wikipedia's figures are for 2025 and earlier (the standard deduction, the $10,000 SALT cap). Personal tax amounts are
  worked out by the calculator from `src/data/tax_rules.yaml`, not from these pages.
