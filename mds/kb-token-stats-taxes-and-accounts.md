# Knowledge-base token statistics: taxes-and-accounts

Measurements of the `taxes-and-accounts` source pages, a retrieval test of two chunk limits and an answer-quality test of the same
limits. The design these numbers feed into is [rag-data-design.md](rag-data-design.md); all categories side by side:
[kb-eval-summary.md](kb-eval-summary.md). Generated by `python -m scripts.kb_report --category taxes-and-accounts` from the
CSVs in [data/](data/); only the notes and findings are written by hand.

<!-- notes:start -->
**US only (2026-11-25):** the agent is for US tax, so `rag.us_only` (config.yaml) makes the chunker leave out any
section about another country in this category and in retirement (`chunker.foreign_section`: a path that names a
country or region, or a world-tour heading such as "By country" on a page that isn't about the US; "By country > United
States" and US states on US pages are kept). That removed 909 sections from 63 pages (Property tax kept 15 of 97, Capital
gains tax 26 of 127). Three pages that were mostly about other countries were dropped (Value-added tax, Wealth tax,
Income splitting) and 41 US pages added to fill back (Medicaid, Supplemental Security Income, Social Security
Disability Insurance, Social Security Act, Social Security Fairness Act, ERISA, Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation,
Health insurance marketplace, TreasuryDirect, Low-Income Housing Tax Credit, the major tax acts since 1981, ...):
**338 pages, 6,997 indexed sections, none about another country.** The token statistics below measure whole pages (before
the filter); the retrieval and answer tests use the filtered chunks.

**Expanded to 300 pages (2026-11-25):** a test of 34 common personal-tax questions found 12 with no proper page and 8
with only a passing mention, so the category was grown to 300. Candidates were the article members of Wikipedia's US tax
categories (United States federal income tax, Taxation in the United States and its subcategories, Tax credits, Tax
terms, Payroll, Property taxes, Tax avoidance, IRS, Tax forms, ...: 1,392 pages) plus titles picked by hand (filing,
credits and deductions, accounts, investments, small business, estate and gift, state and local, IRS processes, recent
laws such as the One Big Beautiful Bill Act). People, court cases, companies, single states and other countries were left
out; each page was fetched and checked to exist, not be a stub (≥ 300 words) or a disambiguation page, and not already be
in an indexed category; 50 of the least useful (tax software brands, policy institutes, older acts, general economics)
were then dropped to reach 300. The four estate, gift, generation-skipping and inheritance tax pages are also in
goal-planning, which is not indexed. Questions q21–q62 were written from the new pages' leads.
After the re-test, 24 of the 34 coverage questions found the right page first (14 before); still weak: estimated
quarterly tax, selling a home, how dividends and savings interest are taxed, Social Security benefits, crypto, student
loan interest, bonuses, rental income, RSUs. Some general pages (Capital gains tax, Exit tax, Value-added tax) bring
other countries' sections, which can outrank US ones.

**First measurement (2026-11-24):** 21 pages, the original 15 plus six added for questions about a person's own
income tax (Property tax, State and local tax deduction, Itemized deduction, Federal Insurance Contributions Act, Tax rate,
Alternative minimum tax). "Self-employment tax" and "Net investment income tax" were checked but resolve to general pages
(Self-employment; Affordable Care Act tax provisions), so they were left out; the NIIT is covered by Capital gains tax in
the United States › Additional taxes, and self-employment tax by FICA › Self-employed people.
Questions: q01–q20 were written from the pages, q21–q22 are out of scope. Indexed the same day (`rag.categories`).

- **Sample:** the 338 `taxes-and-accounts` pages in [kb_topics.yaml](../src/data/kb_topics.yaml), full Wikipedia text (not
  truncated), saved in [tests/data/kb_snapshot/taxes-and-accounts/](../tests/data/kb_snapshot/taxes-and-accounts/).
- **Tokenizer:** `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, the embedding model, without `[CLS]`/`[SEP]`.
- **Raw vs clean:** "raw" is the text as `WikiClient.get_page()` returns it. "Clean" has each MathML formula dump collapsed
  to inline LaTeX; all section and paragraph figures use clean text.
- **Section** = text under one heading (`##`–`#####`), excluding its subsections. **Paragraph** = one line of the source
  text; list items count as separate paragraphs.

Data: [taxes-and-accounts_docs.csv](data/taxes-and-accounts_docs.csv), [taxes-and-accounts_sections.csv](data/taxes-and-accounts_sections.csv), [taxes-and-accounts_paragraphs.csv](data/taxes-and-accounts_paragraphs.csv).
