# Knowledge-base token statistics: goal-planning

Measurements of the `goal-planning` source pages, a retrieval test of two chunk limits and an answer-quality test of the same
limits. The design these numbers feed into is [rag-data-design.md](rag-data-design.md); all categories side by side:
[kb-eval-summary.md](kb-eval-summary.md). Generated by `python -m scripts.kb_report --category goal-planning` from the
CSVs in [data/](data/); only the notes and findings are written by hand.

<!-- notes:start -->
**First measurement (2026-10-02):** 150 pages, the original 15 plus 135 added. How the 135 were chosen:
- From a curated list of 234 candidate titles around personal goals: planning and advice, saving behaviour, estate and
  legacy, family and life events, paying for education, insurance, and buying a home.
- Each page was fetched with `WikiClient.get_page()` and checked to exist, not be a disambiguation page or stub
  (≥ 300 words), and not resolve to a page already listed in any category. 22 failed: 7 missing, 9 stubs, 6 duplicates.
- Of the 212 that passed, 77 were left out by hand: pages that belong to a category still at 15 pages (debt relief →
  budgeting-and-saving; retirement age, annuities, Medicare → retirement; property tax, ISA → taxes-and-accounts), pages
  too general to be about a personal goal (e.g. Entrepreneurship, Life expectancy, Child care), and schemes specific to
  the UK or Canada. Companies, people and "List of…" pages were not candidates.

One of the original 15 is a duplicate: "Dollar cost averaging" resolves to **Dollar-cost averaging**, which
etfs-and-mutual-funds also lists (and has indexed). It is still counted here; it should be dropped from one of the two
before this category is indexed.

Questions: q01–q30 were written from the pages and q31–q32 are out of scope. After the first retrieval run, q02, q10,
q16 and q29 gained correct sections that other pages answer (e.g. "Probate › United States › Avoiding probate" for
q16, and Insurance's "Limited consumer benefits", which advises high deductibles, for q29).

**Model:** this generation run used **`gpt-4o`** (`llm.models.openai` in config.yaml), not the `gpt-4o-mini` named in
the generated text below and used for the earlier categories, so its scores are not strictly comparable with theirs.
<!-- notes:end -->

- **Sample:** the 150 `goal-planning` pages in [kb_topics.yaml](../src/data/kb_topics.yaml), full Wikipedia text (not
  truncated), saved in [tests/data/kb_snapshot/goal-planning/](../tests/data/kb_snapshot/goal-planning/).
- **Tokenizer:** `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, the embedding model, without `[CLS]`/`[SEP]`.
- **Raw vs clean:** "raw" is the text as `WikiClient.get_page()` returns it. "Clean" has each MathML formula dump collapsed
  to inline LaTeX; all section and paragraph figures use clean text.
- **Section** = text under one heading (`##`–`#####`), excluding its subsections. **Paragraph** = one line of the source
  text; list items count as separate paragraphs.

Data: [goal-planning_docs.csv](data/goal-planning_docs.csv), [goal-planning_sections.csv](data/goal-planning_sections.csv), [goal-planning_paragraphs.csv](data/goal-planning_paragraphs.csv).
