# Knowledge-base token statistics: etfs-and-mutual-funds

Measurements of the `etfs-and-mutual-funds` source pages, a retrieval test of two chunk limits and an answer-quality test of the same
limits. The design these numbers feed into is [rag-data-design.md](rag-data-design.md); all categories side by side:
[kb-eval-summary.md](kb-eval-summary.md). Generated by `python -m scripts.kb_report --category etfs-and-mutual-funds` from the
CSVs in [data/](data/); only the notes and findings are written by hand.

<!-- notes:start -->
**History:** the category started with 15 titles; **35 were added on 2026-10-01** to reach **50 pages** (fund companies and
providers, fund types and fees, regulation, and related products such as ETNs, hedge funds and commodity pools). Each new title
was checked: page exists, not a disambiguation page, and not a redirect to a page already in any category (for example
"Cost basis" was dropped because it is already in another category). Pages are saved in full under
`src/data/raw/wikipedia/etfs-and-mutual-funds/`.

**Tests:** the same retrieval and answer-quality tests as `basics`, `bonds` and `stocks`: 33 answerable questions written from the
pages (10 short, 9 medium, 10 long and 4 very long expected sections) and 4 out-of-scope ones, run against max 512 and max 250. Answers
and judging use `gpt-4o-mini`, so scores may be lenient.

**Question set changes:** after the first retrieval run, q17 gained `mutual-fund :: Share classes` and q33 gained Morningstar's *Products and
services* sections, because they answer those questions directly (first run: hit@5 32 of 33 at both limits, q33 missed; after: 33 of 33).
The category was **indexed on 2026-10-01** (added to `rag.categories`; 869 chunks at max 250).
<!-- notes:end -->

- **Sample:** the 50 `etfs-and-mutual-funds` pages in [kb_topics.yaml](../src/data/kb_topics.yaml), full Wikipedia text (not
  truncated), saved in [tests/data/kb_snapshot/etfs-and-mutual-funds/](../tests/data/kb_snapshot/etfs-and-mutual-funds/).
- **Tokenizer:** `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, the embedding model, without `[CLS]`/`[SEP]`.
- **Raw vs clean:** "raw" is the text as `WikiClient.get_page()` returns it. "Clean" has each MathML formula dump collapsed
  to inline LaTeX; all section and paragraph figures use clean text.
- **Section** = text under one heading (`##`–`#####`), excluding its subsections. **Paragraph** = one line of the source
  text; list items count as separate paragraphs.

Data: [etfs-and-mutual-funds_docs.csv](data/etfs-and-mutual-funds_docs.csv), [etfs-and-mutual-funds_sections.csv](data/etfs-and-mutual-funds_sections.csv), [etfs-and-mutual-funds_paragraphs.csv](data/etfs-and-mutual-funds_paragraphs.csv).

## Generation test: answer quality

`python -m scripts.eval_generation --category etfs-and-mutual-funds`: for each question and limit, the top-5 chunks go to `gpt-4o-mini`
(the app's model, temperature 0) with the instruction to answer only from them (≤ 150 words) or say they don't cover
the question. `gpt-4o-mini` then judges the answer against the chunks and the question's `reference` / `key_points`.
Because the same model writes and judges, scores may be lenient.

| Metric | Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | 1–5 | Does the answer address the question asked? |
| Faithfulness | 0–1 | Share of the answer's claims supported by the retrieved chunks (hallucination check); not scored for refusals |
| Correctness | 1–5 | Do the answer's facts agree with the reference answer? |
| Completeness | 0–1 | Share of the reference key points covered; 0 for a refused answerable question |

Refusals ("the sources don't cover this") are detected from the answer text. Raw results, with every answer, unsupported
claim and judge reason: [eval_etfs-and-mutual-funds_generation.csv](data/eval_etfs-and-mutual-funds_generation.csv), [eval_etfs-and-mutual-funds_generation_summary.csv](data/eval_etfs-and-mutual-funds_generation_summary.csv).

**What gets cut off.** The embedding model reads only the first 512 tokens of a chunk, so a section longer than that would lose
its tail if it were embedded whole.
- **39 of 507 sections (7.7%) are 512 tokens or longer.** Embedded whole, **8,979 of the 112,181 section tokens (8.0%)**
  would sit past the limit and never be searchable. The chunker splits these instead, so nothing is cut: at max 512 the
  longest chunk is 502 tokens, at max 250 it is 248, and no chunk in either run exceeds 512.
- **Max 512:** 569 chunks, 40 sections split. **Max 250:** 869 chunks (+53%), **172 sections split** (167 sections are over
  250 tokens on their own; a few more cross the limit once the title prefix is added). Splitting is the price of the smaller limit:
  each answer gets shorter chunks, but long sections now span several of them.
- **Short sections stay whole and are never merged:** 232 of 507 sections (46%) are under 150 tokens and 65 are under 50.
  They are as thin as in the other categories, so retrieval of these is what a later test should look at.
