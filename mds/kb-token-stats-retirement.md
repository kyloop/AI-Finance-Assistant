# Knowledge-base token statistics: retirement

Measurements of the `retirement` source pages, a retrieval test of two chunk limits and an answer-quality test of the same
limits. The design these numbers feed into is [rag-data-design.md](rag-data-design.md); all categories side by side:
[kb-eval-summary.md](kb-eval-summary.md). Generated by `python -m scripts.kb_report --category retirement` from the
CSVs in [data/](data/); only the notes and findings are written by hand.

- **Sample:** the 15 `retirement` pages in [kb_topics.yaml](../src/data/kb_topics.yaml), full Wikipedia text (not
  truncated), saved in [tests/data/kb_snapshot/retirement/](../tests/data/kb_snapshot/retirement/).
- **Tokenizer:** `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, the embedding model, without `[CLS]`/`[SEP]`.
- **Raw vs clean:** "raw" is the text as `WikiClient.get_page()` returns it. "Clean" has each MathML formula dump collapsed
  to inline LaTeX; all section and paragraph figures use clean text.
- **Section** = text under one heading (`##`–`#####`), excluding its subsections. **Paragraph** = one line of the source
  text; list items count as separate paragraphs.

Data: [retirement_docs.csv](data/retirement_docs.csv), [retirement_sections.csv](data/retirement_sections.csv), [retirement_paragraphs.csv](data/retirement_paragraphs.csv).

## Generation test: answer quality

`python -m scripts.eval_generation --category retirement`: for each question and limit, the top-5 chunks go to `gpt-4o-mini`
(the app's model, temperature 0) with the instruction to answer only from them (≤ 150 words) or say they don't cover
the question. `gpt-4o-mini` then judges the answer against the chunks and the question's `reference` / `key_points`.
Because the same model writes and judges, scores may be lenient.

| Metric | Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | 1–5 | Does the answer address the question asked? |
| Faithfulness | 0–1 | Share of the answer's claims supported by the retrieved chunks (hallucination check); not scored for refusals |
| Correctness | 1–5 | Do the answer's facts agree with the reference answer? |
| Completeness | 0–1 | Share of the reference key points covered; 0 for a refused answerable question |

Refusals ("the sources don't cover this") are detected from the answer text. Raw results, with every answer, unsupported
claim and judge reason: [eval_retirement_generation.csv](data/eval_retirement_generation.csv), [eval_retirement_generation_summary.csv](data/eval_retirement_generation_summary.csv).

## Findings

<!-- findings:start -->
- 15 of 16 answerable questions find an expected section in the top 5 at max 250 (14 at max 512); max 250 is better
  here (MRR 0.865 vs 0.786).
- The miss, q16 ("Do I pay tax on investment gains inside a Roth IRA?"), retrieves Roth IRA › Advantages and
  Disadvantages, which also say the account's growth is tax-free; the expected sections were the lead and Differences
  from a Traditional IRA. Counted as a miss, but the answer is still covered.
- Completeness (0.88) is the lowest of all categories: several references list three key points from long (XL)
  sections, and a 250-token piece holds only part of them.
- Out-of-scope questions score 0.723 and 0.718, above `rag.min_score`; both were declined in the generation test.
